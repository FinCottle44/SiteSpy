# Future Growth — When Revenue Justifies It

Things that add credibility and open doors to larger clients, but aren't worth the cost or effort at the one-tenant stage.

---

## 1. VAT Registration

- [ ] Mandatory once turnover exceeds £90k in a rolling 12-month period
- [ ] Consider voluntary registration earlier if AWS costs are significant (you can reclaim VAT on business expenses)
- [ ] Adds 20% to your invoices but most B2B construction clients reclaim it anyway, so it's neutral to them

**When:** Either when you hit the threshold, or when AWS bills make reclaiming worthwhile.

---

## 2. Cyber Essentials Certification

- [ ] UK government-backed cybersecurity standard
- [ ] Self-assessment version is ~£300–500
- [ ] Covers: firewalls, secure config, access control, malware protection, patching
- [ ] Required for some government-adjacent construction contracts (schools, hospitals, social housing)

**When:** Before pitching to tier-1 contractors or public sector projects.

---

## 3. Accountant / Bookkeeper

- [ ] Hire a small-business accountant for year-end filings and tax planning
- [ ] ~£100–200/month for a Ltd company with straightforward affairs
- [ ] They'll handle Corporation Tax return, annual accounts, and payroll if you pay yourself via PAYE

**When:** From day one of the Ltd ideally, but definitely before the first year-end filing deadline (9 months after year-end).

---

## 4. Contractor Agreements (if you hire help)

- [ ] If you engage other developers (like your frontend dev), have a written agreement covering: IP assignment, confidentiality, payment terms, data handling
- [ ] Off-the-shelf templates from Docue or Rocket Lawyer are fine

**When:** Before you pay anyone to write code or access client data.

---

## 5. ISO 27001 / SOC 2

- [ ] Formal information security management certification
- [ ] Expensive and time-consuming (£10k+ and 6+ months for a small org)
- [ ] Only worth it if you're selling to enterprises with formal procurement processes

**When:** Probably never at current scale. Revisit if you land a client with 50+ sites or a framework agreement.

---

## 6. Directors & Officers Insurance

- [ ] Protects you personally as a company director from claims of mismanagement
- [ ] ~£100–200/year
- [ ] Overkill for a single-director company with one client, but cheap peace of mind if revenue grows

**When:** When the company has meaningful revenue or multiple clients.
