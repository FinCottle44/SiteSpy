## How to use

Work through the files in order. Each item has a checkbox — tick them off as you go. Don't jump to file 3 or 4 until 1 and 2 are done.

## Note

This is not legal advice. For contracts and data protection, consider a one-off consultation with a solicitor who understands tech/SaaS (expect £300–500 for a review of your agreements). The investment pays for itself the first time a client pushes back on terms.
